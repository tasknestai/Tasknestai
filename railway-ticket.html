Guidance for booking your own ticket

You book on the official IRCTC website or app using your own IRCTC account. We guide you through the steps and help you avoid common mistakes. You enter your own login, OTP and payment details yourself.

1

Search and choose

Find trains, compare classes and check seat availability for your date on IRCTC.

2

Check names, age and gender, and enter them correctly. Details must match your travel ID.

3

Tatkal rules

Understand Tatkal timing and rules on IRCTC. Availability is decided by IRCTC and no confirmed seat is promised.

4

Cancellation and refund

Learn how cancellation and refund work under IRCTC rules for your ticket type.

What we do not do

TaskNest AI does not use automation, scripts or booking bots. We do not bypass captchas, hold tickets or resell them. IRCTC's terms say its website and apps are for personal, non-commercial use and prohibit automation and scripting software, and they bar booking tickets for commercial resale.

Selling tickets can be a criminal offence.

Under Section 143 of the Railways Act, unauthorised business of procuring and supplying railway tickets is punishable. Do not use personal IRCTC accounts to buy tickets for resale. If you want to book as a business, check IRCTC's official rules for authorised agents.

Keep control of your personal information.

Never share passwords or OTPs. Complete authentication yourself on the official portal. Start with your requirement only, not document images. Read our privacy policy and share only what is needed for the agreed application.

Questions about Train ticket guidance

Is TaskNest AI an IRCTC agent?

No. TaskNest AI is an independent private guidance service. We are not IRCTC, Indian Railways or an authorised IRCTC agent. We do not issue tickets.

What does the ₹40 charge cover?

The listed charge is ₹40 for booking guidance. Train fare, IRCTC charges and any payment-gateway fees are separate and paid by you to IRCTC. Confirm the total before paying.

Can you guarantee a confirmed or Tatkal ticket?

No. Seat availability, waiting list and Tatkal quota are controlled by IRCTC. We do not promise a confirmed seat or a time.

Do you use bots or auto-booking software?

No. IRCTC prohibits automation and scripting software, and we do not use it. You book in your own account.

Can I cancel and get a refund?

Cancellation and refund follow IRCTC and Indian Railways rules for your ticket type. We can guide you through the process, but the refund decision is IRCTC's.

Should I share my password or OTP?

No. Never share passwords or OTPs with TaskNest AI or anyone else. Complete any authentication yourself on the official portal. This website does not accept document uploads, submit applications or take payments.

Can I apply directly without TaskNest AI?

Yes. You can use the official portal yourself. TaskNest AI assistance is optional and our private service fee is not a government fee.
