Re-registration FAQs

What is IGNOU re-registration?

Re-registration means registering for the next year or next semester of your existing IGNOU programme. It is done only on IGNOU's official re-registration portal. Missing it can delay your programme by a semester or a year.

Does TaskNest AI do my re-registration for me?

We provide independent private assistance - subject guidance, form preparation and review. We are not affiliated with IGNOU. Login, OTP authentication and fee payment are done by you on the official portal.

What is the charge?

The listed TaskNest AI service fee is ₹50 for re-registration assistance. It is not a university fee. IGNOU's re-registration fee (and any late fee) is paid separately by you on the official portal.

When is the last date for re-registration?

Dates are announced by IGNOU on the official re-registration portal for each session and can be extended. Check the portal for the current session - we do not publish deadlines because they change.

Do I need to share my OTP or password?

No. Never share OTPs or passwords with TaskNest AI or anyone else. The OTP comes only to your registered mobile number; complete the login yourself.

What is ABC ID and is it compulsory?

ABC (Academic Bank of Credits) ID is created through the official process using Aadhaar authentication. We guide you through the official steps; the ID is generated by the government system, not by us.

My payment debited but the portal did not update. What now?

IGNOU's own instruction is to wait a day, check the payment status and not pay again immediately. If a double payment happens for the same application, one is refunded. Keep the payment proof.

Is my admission confirmed after re-registration?

Re-registration confirmation appears on the official portal after IGNOU processes it. TaskNest AI does not guarantee confirmation or any timeline.
