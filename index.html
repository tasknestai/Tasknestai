Before you start

Common questions

Where can I see the price?

Choose the service you need to open its page. Pricing depends on the work and requirements; ask for a service-specific quote if a rate is not listed. Confirm assistance charges and any official fees separately before payment.

Is TaskNest AI a government or IGNOU website?

No. This is an independent private assistance service, not a government portal or an IGNOU office. Eligibility, approvals, fees and deadlines must be checked with the relevant official source.

What should I send in my first enquiry?

Send only the service name and a short description. Do not send Aadhaar or PAN copies, bank details, passwords or OTPs in the first message. Ask why any document is needed, how it will be used, and how it will be deleted before sharing it.

Can an approval or completion date be guaranteed?

Official approvals and processing dates cannot be guaranteed. For other work, confirm the scope and expected turnaround before proceeding.
